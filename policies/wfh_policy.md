# Policy 3: Work From Home Policy

## Section 1: Eligibility
Confirmed employees in Accounts, Sales and HR may work from home. Production roles and employees on probation are not eligible.

## Section 2: Limit
At most 4 WFH days per calendar month, applied at least 1 day in advance.

## Section 3: Availability
On WFH days employees must be reachable on phone and chat from 10:00 to 17:00.

## Section 4: Holidays
WFH is not allowed on the working day just before or just after a public holiday.
