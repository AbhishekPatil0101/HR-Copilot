# Policy 1: Leave Policy

## Section 1: Leave types
Every confirmed employee gets 8 Casual Leave (CL), 6 Sick Leave (SL) and 15 Earned Leave (EL) per calendar year. Employees on probation get CL and SL only.

## Section 2: Applying for leave
CL must be applied at least 1 day in advance, and at most 3 CL days can be taken in a row. EL must be applied at least 7 days in advance. SL can be applied on the same day.

## Section 3: Carry forward
Unused EL carries forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 30 days. Unused CL and SL lapse on 31 December.

## Section 4: Encashment
EL above the 30-day carry-forward limit is paid out at the end of the year at the basic daily rate.

## Section 5: Medical certificate
SL of more than 2 days in a row needs a medical certificate, uploaded within 3 days of returning.

## Section 6: Sandwich rule
If leave is taken on both a Friday and the following Monday, the weekend in between also counts as leave.
