# Policy 2: Attendance Policy

## Section 1: Shift and grace time
Each employee's shift starts at their shift_start. A check-in up to 15 minutes after shift start is on time.

## Section 2: Late marks
A check-in more than 15 minutes after shift start is a late mark. Every 3 late marks in a calendar month deduct 0.5 day of CL; if no CL is left, 0.5 day of pay is deducted.

## Section 3: Working hours
8 hours or more between check-in and check-out is a full day. 4 to under 8 hours is a half day. Under 4 hours, or no punch with no approved or pending leave, is an absence.

## Section 4: Missed punch
A day with a check-in but no check-out must be regularised through HR within 3 working days, or it counts as a half day. Each employee can regularise at most 3 days a month.

## Section 5: Overtime
Time worked beyond 9 hours on a working day is overtime and needs manager approval to be paid.
